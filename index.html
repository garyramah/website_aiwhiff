AIWHIFF

Catching the scent of what didn't make it.

A living ledger of the casualties of the AI revolution: the projects, startups, and products that shut down, went bankrupt, got sunset, or got absorbed — with the cause of death and the lesson, for each one.

What counts as a casualty. A shutdown, bankruptcy, discontinuation, or sunset of a notable AI project, startup, or product. When a standalone product is folded into a bigger one, that counts as a death of the standalone thing. Damaged-but-alive products and rolled-back launches go under Walking Wounded, not the graveyard. Causes are tagged: cost, trust, absorption, no market, or user revolt. Entries are summarized from public reporting, not reproduced. Seeded from the TechCrunch AI graveyard list (updated October 24, 2026). Last updated October 25, 2026. Draft — not yet deployed.

THE DEADConfirmed shutdowns, bankruptcies, and absorptions, newest first.

Character.ai — Character.ai

Bankruptcy filed early October 2026 · Founded 2021
Trust

Companion-chatbot platform felled by lawsuits alleging its chatbots contributed to teen suicides and self-harm, a Disney cease-and-desist over its characters, and a ban on open-ended chats with under-18s. Google's reported $2.7B license-and-rehire deal in 2023 was the real exit; bankruptcy is the epilogue. Disney is the opening bidder at $20M for some of the IP; the app still runs while the sale closes, expected late November to early December 2026.

Lesson: Companion AI aimed at kids carries existential legal risk, and no valuation survives it.

ChatGPT Atlas — OpenAI

Discontinued August 9, 2026 · Lived less than a year
No market

AI-powered web browser that never justified itself as a standalone product.

Lesson: The browser is a distribution moat, not a product.

Relay — Relay

Shut down August 2026
Absorption

AI workflow-automation tool positioned as a Zapier alternative, shut down entirely.

Lesson: Agent workflow tools die when the platforms they automate absorb the capability.

Huxe — ex-Google NotebookLM developers

Shut down May 2026
Absorption

AI audio app that turned text into podcast-style audio, squeezed out as larger platforms like Spotify offered the same features.

Lesson: A feature the platforms can copy is not a company.

Yupp — Yupp

Shut down March 2026
No market

Free playground for comparing 800+ AI models side by side, with voting and crypto earnings. Never found product-market fit.

Lesson: Comparison is a feature, not a destination.

Sora video-sharing platform — OpenAI

Shut down March 2026
Cost

Video-sharing platform closed over high operating costs and user-retention problems.

Lesson: Video generation economics punish standalone apps.

Operator — OpenAI

Folded into ChatGPT, 2026
Absorption

Web-browsing AI agent folded into the main product; the standalone agent ceased to exist.

Lesson: Agents become features, not products.

DALL-E, standalone — OpenAI

Shrunk into ChatGPT, 2026
Absorption

Image generation moved inside ChatGPT; DALL-E as a standalone product withered.

Lesson: When the model host owns the interface, the tool brand dies.

Notion Mail — Notion

Shutdown September 2026 · Launched April 2025
No market

AI-focused email product closed because users preferred their own separate AI agents for email.

Lesson: The agent layer eats the app layer.

Humane AI Pin — Humane

Business shut February 2025 · Raised $230M
Trust

Hyped AI wearable plagued by poor performance and a battery fire risk on its charging case. HP acquired most of the assets for $116M.

Lesson: Hardware has to be ten times better, not just AI inside.

Figgs AI — Figgs AI

Ran 2023–2024 · 1M+ users
Cost

Customizable AI characters to create and chat with. Shut down because keeping the free service running cost too much.

Lesson: Free AI companionship has no business model at scale.

WALKING WOUNDEDAlive, but damaged — delayed, backlash-hit, or rolled back.

Apple Siri AI — Apple

Delayed since the 2024 Apple Intelligence unveiling
Trust

Repeatedly delayed; a $250M settlement over iPhone 16 AI marketing claims followed. The AI-powered Siri finally appeared in the iOS 27 beta in July 2026 and began rolling out to English-language users in October 2026.

Lesson: Announcing AI before it exists is a liability.

Microsoft Recall — Microsoft

Announced Build 2024 · Delayed nearly a year
Trust

AI "photographic memory" for Windows PCs met immediate privacy and security backlash. Controversy persists after a researcher built a tool extracting data Recall had captured.

Lesson: Capability without consent is a casualty in slow motion.

Rabbit R1 — Rabbit

Unveiled CES January 2024 · 100,000 units sold
User revolt

AI companion judged unfinished and unreliable at launch. Still being updated; Rabbit has announced "Project Cyberdeck," a portable device for vibe-coding.

Lesson: Shipping the demo is not shipping the product.

ChatGPT super-app redesign — OpenAI

Launched July 9, 2026 · Rolled back
User revolt

Redesign that turned ChatGPT into a "super app" with Chat, Codex, and Work modes, rolled back after user criticism.

Lesson: Users punish redesigns that move their tools.

THE PATTERNWhat the ledger says so far.

Most of these did not die on technical merit. They died on cost, retention, trust, or absorption into a bigger product. The graveyard's through-line: the model hosts and the platforms eat everything that cannot defend a moat — and trust failures kill faster than technical ones.

Running count: 11 dead, 4 walking wounded, as of October 25, 2026. About 42% of AI initiatives are ultimately abandoned by their corporate parents (S&P Global Market Intelligence, via TechCrunch).
